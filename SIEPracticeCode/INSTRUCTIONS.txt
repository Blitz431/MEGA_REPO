SIE QUIZZER - SETUP & USAGE GUIDE
====================================


FIRST-TIME SETUP
-----------------
1. Install Python 3.9+ from https://python.org (if not already installed)
   That's it. Everything else is handled automatically.

   Optional — create a Desktop shortcut:
      powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File create_shortcut.ps1


LAUNCHING THE QUIZZER
----------------------
   Option A:  Double-click "SIE Quizzer" on the Desktop
   Option B:  Double-click "run_quiz.bat" in this folder
   Option C:  Open a terminal here and run:  python quiz.py


STARTUP SCREEN
--------------
When you launch the quizzer it shows your progress dashboard — a table of
every subsection showing how many questions are in the bank and your
right / wrong counts. Then it asks you to pick a mode.


MODES
------
F  Full Exam Mode
     Simulates the real SIE exam: 75 questions drawn from all four
     sections using the official exam distribution:
       Section 1 (Knowledge of Capital Markets)          12 questions
       Section 2 (Understanding Products & Their Risks)  33 questions
       Section 3 (Trading, Accounts & Prohibited Acts)   23 questions
       Section 4 (Regulatory Framework)                   7 questions
     After finishing you see a section-by-section breakdown and a
     PASS / FAIL indicator (passing is 72%).

S  Study Mode
     Choose exactly which subsections to study.
     1. Pick a main section (1-4) or "A" for all sections.
     2. Toggle individual subsections on/off in the checklist.
     3. Set a target question count.
        - If the bank is short, the quizzer auto-fills from other
          subsections in the SAME main section (never crosses sections).
        - If the bank has more than you need, it randomly samples down.
     Adaptive learning is ON by default (type A to toggle it off).

W  Weak Areas Mode
     Shows only the questions you have answered incorrectly before
     (WRONG > 0). Great for targeted review before exam day.


ADAPTIVE LEARNING
------------------
When adaptive learning is ON (the default in Study Mode and always in
Full Exam Mode), questions you have answered wrong more often get a
higher chance of appearing. Questions you consistently get right are
shown less. This focuses your practice where you need it most.

You can toggle adaptive learning ON/OFF at the target-count prompt
by typing A.


MENUS & NAVIGATION
-------------------
Checklist menus:
   <number>   toggle that item on/off
   A          select all
   C          clear all
   Enter      confirm your selection

Question answering:
   1 / 2 / 3 / 4   pick your answer (choices are shuffled every time)

Anywhere:
   H or ?   show the in-app help screen
   Q        go back / return to main menu
   Ctrl+C   exit the program immediately


TRACKING & STATS
-----------------
After every answer the quizzer updates three columns in
"QUESTION BANK AND TRACKER.xlsx":
   # Of times asked   incremented by 1
   RIGHT              incremented if your answer was correct
   WRONG              incremented if your answer was wrong

A session summary is also appended to "session_log.csv" so you can
track your improvement over time.

IMPORTANT: Close Excel before running the quizzer, otherwise the
quizzer cannot write your stats back to the file.


ADDING QUESTIONS
-----------------
Open "QUESTION BANK AND TRACKER.xlsx" and add rows in this format:

   Col A (Section)         Subsection code, e.g.  2.2
   Col B (Question)        Full question text
   Col C (A)               Answer choice A
   Col D (B)               Answer choice B
   Col E (C)               Answer choice C
   Col F (D)               Answer choice D
   Col G (Correct Answer)  The correct letter: A, B, C, or D
   Col H (# Of times asked) Start at 0
   Col I (RIGHT)            Start at 0
   Col J (WRONG)            Start at 0

Section codes follow this structure:
   1.1  Regulatory Entities, Agencies & Market Participants
   1.2  Market Structure
   1.3  Economic Factors
   2.1  Equity Securities
   2.2  Debt Securities
   2.3  Packaged Products
   2.4  Options
   2.5  Alternative Investments & Other Products
   3.1  Trading Securities
   3.2  Customer Accounts & Compliance
   3.3  Prohibited Activities
   4.1  SROs and Regulatory Bodies
   4.2  Registration & Licensing
   4.3  Key Regulations & Rules
